The official logo artwork is NOT redesigned or recreated by this project — please
drop the real logo file supplied by Druva at /public/logo.png. Until you do, the

on the header and footer. If your logo has a white background and you want it to sit
cleanly on the dark footer, export a white/transparent version and save it as
logo-white.png.
